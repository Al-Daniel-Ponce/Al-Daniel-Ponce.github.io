A few projects I've made

Website Game

RegEx Game & Website

An interactive web-based quiz game designed to help users practice Regular Expressions (RegEx). The website generates randomized questions for each session, allowing players to improve their pattern-matching skills through engaging and challenging quizzes while receiving instant feedback on their answers.

Rage Game

Trapped Pixel

is a rage-style platformer game designed to challenge players’ skill, patience, and perseverance through precise controls and difficult levels. Developed using Unity 6 and C#, with SQLite for storing player progress and performance data, the game focuses on responsive gameplay, fair difficulty, and rewarding mastery.

Mobile App

I owe U

A Flutter-based mobile application developed with Dart that helps users track and manage debts. Users can record who owes them, monitor amounts, and keep track of payment status for easier and more organized debt management.

Mobile App

Drowzeee

A Flutter-based mobile application developed with Dart that tracks users’ sleep duration and helps them explore the possible meanings behind their dreams. The app combines sleep tracking with dream interpretation to provide a more engaging and personalized sleep experience.
